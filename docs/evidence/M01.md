## Tuning values the master leaves open (M01 defaults, all in `src/sim/data/rules.ts`)

- **Water and stone bands:** deep sea below elevation 64, sea level 90, upland at 215 and above, stone at 140 and above with 400 units per cell.
- **Noise:** contrast ×2.
- **Fertility:** base values by biome, plus 0.6 × moisture and a coastal +200, capped at 1000.
- **Biomass:** capacities by biome.
- **Start spacing:** at least 8 wrapped steps. Candidate sites need two adjacent fertility-1000 parcels, which is the §16.5 guaranteed opening, and woodland and stone within two rings.
- **Forestry:** 10 biomass per TIMBER. Worked radius for forestry and quarry is 2.
- **Famine:** deaths are 10% of unfed milli-population per turn, after the §6.1 two-turn grace period.
- **Queue:** at most 8 construction items.
- **Legitimacy:** starts at 500.
- **Cancel refund:** cancelling refunds reserved materials only if no work has been applied.

## Known limits (not claimed)

- **No God.** God records, orders, footprint pathing and observation are M02.
- **Partial world generation.** Emigration, portable settlements, founding, rivers, edges, drainage, start fairness (§3.3 15% rule) and the God habitat graph belong to later milestones.
- **No persistence storage.** IndexedDB persistence, autosave rotation and compressed `.cgsave` export are not implemented. Only the pure envelope exists.
- **The scripted driver is not the AI.** Its identical openings give identical populations (6724 milli after 30 turns) across all settlements. The runs differ in terrain, biomass and hashes, not in growth.
- **`test:sim` runtime.** It now runs 40 tests, including the 50×2 campaigns (about 18 s).
