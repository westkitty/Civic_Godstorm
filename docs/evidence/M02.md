## Measurements (Node 24.21.0, Apple M1)

- A standard 6-civilization turn went from 28 ms to 12 ms after an exact integer-array fast path in canonical serialisation. The output is byte-identical: the pre- and post-optimization self-check hash is `249947aa…`.
- A route preview through a mostly unknown map takes about 70–100 ms when the SAFE search exhausts the map (reported `NO_PATH`). That is above the §15 God-path p95 target of 60 ms. It is recorded for M12 and not yet optimised.

## M02 defaults the master leaves open (in `src/sim/data/rules.ts` and code)

- **Terrain costs:** hills (elevation 160 and above) and forest cost 2 AP; shallow water is a 2-AP ford; deep water is illegal for PILLAR.
- **Turning:** turns cost 0 AP. The planner weights a turn at 1/16 AP so it never prefers turning over moving.
- **Disturbance:** applied to newly entered cells.
- **Swept-cell hazards:** farm parcels in the swept region need `civilianCollateral` consent; foreign claim rings need `trespass` consent. City cores and other God bodies block movement.
- **BROWSE feeding zone:** the God's own wild cells, excluding farms and foreign claims.
- **Start support:** 20 turns × upkeep × 10 biomass within two rings, with no regrowth credit.
- **Sight:** sight radius only. Hill bonuses and line of sight are not implemented yet.

## Known limits (not claimed)

- **Bodies:** only the Q size-I baseline body exists. S trails, other families, genome choice, start previews and Godform are M06.
- **Commands:** STRIKE, GUARD, SHAPE, CULTIVATE, ASSIST, HIBERNATE, MIGRATE feeding stops and WITHDRAW are M06/M07.
- **Death:** the permanent-death transaction (remains, memorial, succession) is M05/M07. A God at zero vital health only stops accepting living commands.
- **Art:** God and city art are `MISSING` placeholders. Terrain is schematic, because CG-R-TERRAIN depends on the unapproved CG-S-ART-DIRECTION.
- **Devices:** touch was tested only in emulation. The physical tablet journey is M10.
- **Presentation:** there is no animation interpolation; the body snaps to committed poses. The narrow layout is a stacked panel, not the M10 bottom sheet.
- **Debug hooks:** `window.__CG_DEBUG__` and the command-log panel exist only with `?debug=1` and are visibly marked "DEBUG TOOLS ENABLED".
