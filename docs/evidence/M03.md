# M03 - First complete God: evidence record

**Verdict: CANDIDATE. Technical gates are met. The milestone is not accepted, because it waits on the human art decision and on two recorded reference conflicts.** This was the primary pass. No repair pass has been used.

## What was delivered

- **Source intake.** Human ruling HR-2026-09-23-01 is recorded in `assets/provenance.json`. Eight B00/B01 sources were copied byte for byte from Arena commit `6f07fd4` to their canonical paths at native resolution. The audit verifies each one's hash, native dimensions, dimension waiver and human approver. The tool is `tools/assets/intake.ts`.
- **Conversion toolchain.** Blender 4.5.14 LTS runs as `bpy` in a project-local venv (`tools/models/requirements.txt`). The cloud container cannot reach download.blender.org.
- **Six GLBs** in `assets/runtime/models/`: TORSO-Q, LOCO-PILLAR, FEED-BROWSE, SENSE-EYE-RING, TAIL-BALANCE and the FORM-Q fixture. Each has three LODs within budget, vertex colour, one opaque non-metal material, and a real skin (the eye band is rigid). Each also has a `.blend` and a `.recipe.json` in `assets/authoring/` and a `.meta.json`. A rerun from the same volumes gives a byte-identical GLB.
- **FORM-Q skeleton.** 28 joints. The seven ordinary poses (idle, walk, turn, feed, brace, rest, sleep) are data in `src/render/god/qPoses.json`, shared by the runtime and the validator.
- **Pipeline.** `tools/models/q-pipeline.sh` runs: volumes, then models, then the pose ground solve, then measurements, then the Khronos validator, then the Blender reimport, then the offline viewers, then the turntables and sheets, then provenance registration.
- **Evidence per model** in `artifacts/inspection/<ID>/c001/`: `viewer.html` (GLB embedded, no network), `turntable.mp4` (144 frames, 1024², H.264), `validation-sheet.png` (2048²), `validation.json`, `measurements.json`, `gltf-validator.json`, `reimport.json`, `reimport-views.png` and `silhouette-overlay.png`. FORM-Q also has `age-injury-sample.png`.
- **Map.** The player's Q God is drawn with the FORM-Q candidate. It is scaled to size I, rotated by heading per §16.2, stands on the terrain, and takes its pose from its order. The God panel labels it "Unapproved candidate". A GLB whose hash differs from its record is refused, and the MISSING placeholder stays.

## Recorded reference conflicts (need a human decision)

1. **FORM-Q top panel is not bilaterally symmetric.** Its mirror-symmetry IoU is 0.711, against 0.996 for the front panel. A symmetric body cannot reach 0.90 against it.
2. **The LIFE-Q prime side panel is perspective-drawn.** It shows 4 separate leg columns at ground level, where an orthographic profile shows 2.

Master §18.3 treats these as non-projectable references. They need an approved corrected reference or fit before the numbers are frozen. Nothing was faked.

## Known limits

- **Eye band.** It reads as a raised flange in three-quarter views rather than a flush brow strap.
- **Head and tail.** They are stubbier than in FORM-Q.
- **Surface finish.** Lighting is smooth and schematic; there is no sculpted detail.
- **Sleep pose.** It is a crouch; the ground solve raised its root.
- **Map placement.** Only the size-I God is shown on the map. Foreign Gods keep the placeholder, because observation carries no body data.
- **Turntable renderer.** It is SwiftShader Chromium, not a GPU.
- **Life stages.** They are not yet driven by simulation age; the stage rules are M06.
