## Reference conflicts, remeasured (not copied from c001)

1. **LIFE-Q r005 depicts a different body than FORM-Q r004.** The sheet draws a domed segmented carapace over the body and a tortoise-like head; FORM-Q r004 and the TORSO-Q sheet have no shell. The prime panel is also drawn with perspective: 3 separate leg columns at 93% of silhouette height, where an orthographic profile shows 2. The old "perspective-drawn side view" conflict **survives**, and a new identity conflict is added. FORM-Q was not reshaped to match it: `hullClip` is off, so LIFE-Q only scores FORM-Q and never shapes it.
2. **FORM-Q top panel.** Remeasured mirror-symmetry IoU about the best body axis (±6 px search): top 0.908, front 0.996, rear 0.995. The c001 figure of 0.711 used a different method and is not reproduced, so strong asymmetry is **not** confirmed on the canonical bytes. The top IoU of 0.751 is mainly a planform/width mismatch (flank and limb outline), not a tail-length mismatch: in the overlay the tail length matches.
