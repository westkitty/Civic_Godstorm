### 17.2 Global art and technical locks

**Art direction:** monumental biological cartography. Broad, designed silhouettes; continuous organic surfaces; restrained faceted/stylized rough materials; readable warm civic forms against cool living terrain. Not photoreal, toy-plastic, voxel, generic fantasy dragon art, painterly UI text or ornamental noise. Gods have coherent mass and ground/water contact; cities look inhabited through layout and movement, not hand-painted people. Civilization motifs are architectural grammar, not historical Earth stereotypes.

**Palette:** charcoal `#161E27`, pale stone `#E1CEAC`, ochre `#B98549`, vegetation `#55745C`, water `#315B75`, biological teal `#49A69D`, restrained organ accent `#E4C86E`. Faction tints are exactly the six emblem-row values. UI background `#14191F`, foreground `#F4ECDE`, danger `#E7675D`. These are base tokens, not a ban on antialiasing/shading. Broad lightness/value ordering must survive grayscale. Emissive biological accents cover <=5% of God projected area and never replace its anatomy.

**Model orientation:** right-handed, +Y up, +Z forward, +X anatomical right. Neutral pose faces +Z; side camera at -X makes forward point toward image right. Front camera is +Z, rear -Z, top +Y with +Z at image top. Apply export transforms; unit scale 1. Root for buildings/props/people is bottom-center at ground contact; ships use waterline-center; equipment uses grip or attachment center; God modules use the root of the anatomical junction, and complete Gods use their logical anchor with a measured centroid offset. These are target conventions to verify after export, not assumed facts about images.

**Source camera/lighting:** orthographic where stated; identical scale across the views of one object; neutral 6500 K broad illumination, no dramatic perspective, depth-of-field, lens effects, silhouette-obscuring shadow or baked environmental effects. Opaque reference background is uniform neutral gray `#808080`. Keep 8% panel safe margins. No labels, captions, watermarks, UI chrome, real-world emblems or invented anatomy inside generated pixels. Required view labels are added in an inspection wrapper, not entrusted to generated text.

**Source formats and normalization:** accepted canonical sources are 8-bit sRGB PNGs at the exact profile dimensions, with an actual native-resolution/provenance record. Images have one image frame; panel count is not animation frame count. Native output is preferred. For orthographic/reference boards only, a native image at least half the target width and height with matching aspect may be normalized once up to 2x using a recorded deterministic resampler; this adds no claimed detail and never proves geometric accuracy. The title requires at least 1536 x 768 native pixels; glyphs at least 512 x 512. Downsampling is allowed; aspect error >1%, cropped anatomy, or more than 2% corrective padding requires revision. Never stretch, invent alpha, or report normalized pixels as native pixels. Preserve raw candidates and hashes. Source sheets have no tileability/UV/normal/roughness requirement because they are references, not runtime texture maps.

**Runtime materials:** vertex-colored, nonmetallic (metallic 0), roughness 0.85 for civic/mineral and 0.75 for organic modules, alpha OPAQUE, one material for ordinary props and at most two per assembled God. Thin membranes may use double-sided opaque material; no transparency workaround that hides broken geometry. No authored albedo/normal/ORM/emission texture set is requested. sRGB colors convert to linear lighting in the renderer; alpha/masks/height/roughness data are non-color. GLB materials have a portable rough-PBR approximation; stylized light quantization belongs to the renderer, with the difference recorded.

**Animation ownership:** Arena supplies shape/posture evidence only. Opus creates actual skeleton+skin and procedural movement; source images contain no baked clips. A true rig has joints, inverse-bind data, normalized weights and observable deformation. A parented hierarchy does not pass. Models do not require named animation clips. Semantic joints/sockets are planned in conversion recipes and discovered/validated in actual exports before use.

**Collision:** all authoritative collision is discrete map domain/footprint/edge logic from Section 16. Meshes have no physics collider requirement. Every selectable runtime instance has an ID-backed proxy and actual measured bounds; a cosmetic overhang cannot extend its damage range. Reference source images have no collision object.

**Approval/rights:** the user approves global style, first God identity, first civic motif and final visual quality. Record candidate/parent revision, generator/tool, date, source dependencies, native/normalized dimensions, terms/provenance basis, hash and decision. No asset is assumed cleared for commercial release solely because it exists. Candidates/rejected references are never copied into the shipping manifest.
