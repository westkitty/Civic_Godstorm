## 1. Executive definition and frozen V1

**Definition:** CIVIC GODSTORM is a turn-based civilization-scale 4X in which the player directly commands a persistent colossal organism while building a society capable of living with, changing, exploiting, resisting or outliving it.

The paired fantasy is direct command of an enormous living God and responsibility for the civilization-shaped consequences. Neither is subordinate. The defining structural difference is that a mobile multi-cell body, its ecological demands and its eventual remains participate in the same territory, infrastructure, population, logistics and diplomatic rules as the rest of the world. A God changes which plans are feasible, not merely their numerical efficiency.

The pillars are physical geography; direct agency; mechanical-and-visible lifelong development; constrained but meaningful procedural morphology; two-way civic dependency; autonomous civilization; permanent death and remains; rare catastrophic encounters; AI parity; legible accumulated history.

### 1.2 Premium-quality contract

The release must make city growth, route use, damage, ecological recovery, God posture, body evolution and historical remains visible on the actual map. A player must locate their God and explain its current order, next resource shortfall and immediate affected territory in one interaction. A screenshot cannot prove this; Section 20 supplies journeys. Asset throughput cannot compensate for shallow mechanics, and a mechanically working primitive creature cannot pass the first-God gate.

## 2. Campaign loop, turns and endings

### 2.1 Campaign phases

**Turns 1-30: coexistence.** The player starts with five population units in one settlement, a small God, one scout, one militia army, a known food source and a safe resting area. Food, roads, scouting and God movement are available immediately. The first dilemma is whether to preserve feeding territory, cultivate civic fields or route commerce around it. The introduction uses a constrained seed and contextual tasks, never a different simulation.

**Turns 31-90: expansion.** Additional settlements, coastal shipping, treaty corridors and alternative food systems appear. At least two distinct supply strategies must support each God recipe. The first major morphological development and the first durable institutions make the civilization visibly different.

**Turns 91-170: geopolitics.** Population and extraction compete with ecological carrying capacity. Migration, sanctuaries, deterrence, service dependency and trade chokepoints matter. More territory is not automatically better: administration, transport losses and local legitimacy constrain expansion.

**Turns 171-240: inheritance.** Aging, accumulated injuries, ossification, mature institutions, technological substitutes and long histories drive choices. The God may remain active, become deliberately less central, hibernate, or die. Civic victory never requires the God to survive.

**Post-God is a state, not a late-game phase.** It can begin on turn 2. All economic, military, settlement, technology and diplomatic systems remain usable. The God command panel becomes a memorial/corpse panel without removing empire management or declaring defeat.

### 2.2 Authoritative turn order

A turn has `PLANNING -> LOCKED -> RESOLVING -> COMMITTED -> PLANNING`. Planning edits an order draft, not committed world state. The player can undo drafts without rewinding discoveries. AI drafts are made from each AI's own observation of the same committed turn. No side reacts to secret orders while planning.

1. Validate all commands against ownership, current observation, available stock, prerequisites and declared consent. Sort by `(civId, actorId, commandSequence)` for validation only; contested physical outcomes use the rules below.
2. Apply accepted treaty responses, policy changes and budget reservations. Newly proposed treaties are delivered for the next planning phase. No movement relies on an unaccepted offer.
3. Compute seasonal climate, ecological replenishment and local hazard warnings from deterministic state. Do not generate an unannounced lethal surprise simply to create drama.
4. Resolve four impulses. In each: collect intentions; adjudicate movement and spatial conflicts; apply actions and terrain/structure damage; resolve simultaneous attacks; update visibility; process displacement and reaction stops. Effects in an impulse read its start snapshot and commit together.
5. Produce goods at surviving workplaces, move eligible trade consignments, consume local household/army/God upkeep, apply shortages, execute population and institution changes, research, healing and development progress. Resource reservations are reconciled; unused reservations return to the owner.
6. Update claims, diplomatic incidents, historic events, victory conditions and the new hash. Commit the turn atomically; only then show `Saved` after persistence has actually committed.

Four impulses are strategic scheduling slots, not a claim that each animation represents 1.25 uninterrupted years. Animation duration and skip settings never change results. Planning remains paused without timers. There is no mandatory clock-speed management.

### 2.3 Movement and collision scheduling

A God has four action points (AP) per turn, spending at most two AP per impulse; armies have six AP, fleets six, scouts eight. Each actor has at most one current major action plus a bounded route queue. Movement consumes terrain/edge AP; at most two edge crossings per actor per impulse. Persistent routes continue next turn without resubmission.

Same-cell armies of the same civilization merge only if the six-company capacity allows it; otherwise the lower initiative move waits. Opposing armies entering the same cell meet at the contested edge and remain at their origin until combat permits entry. Swaps between hostile actors are encounters, not tunneling through one another. Ordinary initiative is a stable per-turn rotation of civilization ID, then actor ID; it is not always a permanent first-player advantage.

God swept footprints have priority over ordinary unit standing space, but not a magical right to pass through another God. Friendly civilians/armies evacuate to a legal adjacent cell before a safe God move; no safe exit makes the order invalid without a separately confirmed destructive passage. Two God swept footprints intersecting cause both to halt at the last legal position. An explicit attack/escalation order is required to turn that halt into God combat. Physical crowding can still close a route or create an incident without war.

### 2.4 Cadence and delegation

Every turn offers God orders and empire decisions. Only threatened or changed objects demand attention. Persistent budgets, citizen job allocation, trade schedules, construction queues and safe route orders reduce repeated clicking. Automation is opt-in and bounded by an explicit policy, with priority `physical legality > player consent boundaries > latest player command > standing automation > hold`. No automatic God attack, trespass, forced feeding on a protected site or starvation march is allowed by default.

The intended steady-state interaction is one consequential God decision every 2-5 turns and 2-4 empire decisions each turn, not six mandatory care buttons per turn. A pacing test measures interaction count and overrides rather than assuming this target has been met.

### 2.5 Endings and defeat

Three public, non-God-exclusive victory paths can end a campaign after turn 120:

- **Commonworld:** a ratified world compact covers at least two-thirds of surviving civilizations and 60% of world population, with fifteen consecutive turns of honored corridor, sanctuary and non-aggression obligations. The signatories share this victory; the human is explicitly told it is cooperative.
- **Continuance:** build the three civic megaprojects, sustain welfare at least 700/1000 and a non-divine share of essential service capacity at least 800/1000 for twenty consecutive turns. The God can be alive, asleep, absent or dead. This is an infrastructure and welfare achievement, not a reward for killing it.
- **Hegemony:** hold at least four of the six original capital sites for ten turns on a six-civilization map, or `ceil(2 * startingCivilizations / 3)` sites on smaller setups, while maintaining at least 600/1000 welfare in occupied populations. Gods cannot occupy cities; armies and actual supply are required.

At turn 240, offer the historical ending, comparative ledger and continuation to turn 1,000. Historical score is `30*welfare + 25*resilience + 20*heritage + 15*treatyReliability + 10*infrastructureCoverage`, with each input normalized to 0..1000 and the result divided by 1000. Heritage is capped unique first-instance civic achievements, not repeated event farming. Resilience measures actual service redundancy, not God death. Tie order is fewer civilian deaths, then equal rank; never hidden randomness. Optional continuation does not add content or new Gods.

Defeat occurs only when the civilization has no settlement, no portable settlement and no recoverable citizen convoy for three turns. God death alone cannot satisfy this rule. A player may concede or continue in non-interactive history inspection after actual civilization defeat.

## 3. World, topology and physical geography

### 3.1 Coordinates and scale

Use axial hex coordinates `(q,r)` over a rectangular offset-index storage layout. Wrap columns east-west, not rows. Distance and pathfinding must evaluate the wrapped shortest alternative. Rendering duplicates only boundary presentation objects; it does not duplicate state or collide a God with its own wrap image.

One world unit (`U`) is a stylized display unit, not one meter. Adjacent cell centers are 32 U apart; hex circumradius is `32 / sqrt(3)` U. A cell represents an abstract region rather than literal distance. House height is 1.5-4 U; a person proxy is 0.45 U; a small/medium/colossal God's primary mass is approximately 12/22/36 U high when its body family permits it. Serpents and mantles are lower but longer/wider. Logical multi-cell occupancy is visible; the God is not just a giant token hovering over unrelated cells.

The map is compressed cartography: people, houses and land distances are not at a common real-world scale. This is declared art direction, not a silent collision-scale mismatch. God mesh plan-view bounds must fit the declared occupied/swept cells with at most 5% decorative overhang; an attack hit is never determined from an unannounced decorative overhang.

### 3.2 Cell and edge state

A cell owns integer elevation 0..255, geology class, temperature band, moisture 0..1000, fertility 0..1000, biomass 0..1000, wildlife 0..1000, water contamination 0..1000, soil disturbance 0..1000, fire state, local mineral reserves, construction parcels, physical occupancies and political claims. Presentation noise is separate from these quantities.

Edges own river flow/width, road grade, bridge capacity/condition, canal width, patrol coverage and treaty corridor membership. Ports connect land settlement nodes to water navigation. Rivers are directed hydrological edges, not painted lines with no supply effect.

Five navigation domains share the map: surface land, shallow water/river, deep water, shallow subsurface and air. There is no separately explorable underground map. Subsurface movement follows geology and affects the same surface cells through stress and subsidence. Air Gods still need legal landing/resting and feeding footprints and cannot remain airborne indefinitely.

### 3.3 Terrain and world generation

V1 biomes are grassland, woodland, boreal forest, wetland, desert, tundra, volcanic upland, coast/shallow sea and deep sea. Elevation and water state modify these, rather than spawning further unlisted biome families.

Generation order: seed streams -> wrapped elevation field -> drainage and outlets -> climate/moisture -> biome assignment -> geology/mineral deposits -> biomass/wildlife capacity -> God habitat graph -> settlement suitability -> starting pair allocation -> connectivity/fairness validation -> decor seed. Noise is integer-table/hash based where it affects gameplay; render-only noise may use floats.

Choose starting God genomes before final start placement. Each start must have a legal initial footprint, a safe rest site, at least twenty turns of reachable ecological support at conservative regeneration, two expansion sites within twelve land or shipping steps, timber/stone access, and a non-divine food path. Aquatic starts must have continuous water access to two ports; burrowers need a connected diggable component; winged starts need two landing sites and surface food access. River-only starts must have channel width compatible with the complete body, not just its head.

For each candidate seed, allocate at most 64 deterministic start attempts. Reject a failed seed with named constraints; try the next candidate seed. After 32 failed world candidates, present a generation failure with seed and diagnostics; never silently violate ecology or use infinite retry. Record the chosen seed and rejection counters.

Map fairness is opportunity, not identical yields. Starting resource/food potential over twenty turns must lie within 15% of the median, after accounting for a God's unavoidable support costs. The player chooses from three legal start previews or accepts a fully random valid start. AI gets the same allocation rules.

### 3.4 Lasting spatial effects

A shared `SpatialImpact` operation receives affected cells/edges, impact type, intensity, source actor and cause event. It applies to God movement, army destruction, mining and disasters. It can change fertility, biomass, elevation, flow capacity, road condition, building integrity, ownership claims and displacement pressure. No God-only scripted terrain bypass exists.

Ordinary heavy passage adds `10 * sizeMass` soil disturbance and damages low-grade roads by the same amount. Safe engineered Godways reduce both by 75%. Feeding removes actual stocks; rest reduces buildable parcels while adding protection/pilgrimage attraction; repeated passage creates a path easement claim. Elevation changes are limited to one terrain step (eight elevation units) per affected cell per action and trigger local drainage repair, not full hydraulic simulation. Depressed coastal cells can flood; sediment can close a shallow channel. A warning preview lists all known structures/cells at risk.

Affected navigation and economy edges increment revision counters. Only dirty connected components and affected routes are recomputed. Event-caused geography keeps its cause ID so history can explain why a harbor moved or a road bends.

## 4. The God: grammar, body and direct command

### 4.1 God record and invariants

A God record contains owner, unique ID, immutable lineage seed, body family, installed module IDs, body size, age, heading, anchor and body trail, navigation domain, occupied cells, tissue/organ health, reserves, fatigue, adaptation evidence, current order, bounded route, automation policy, follower relationship and complete injury/death history. Every movement, attack, feed and modification is an ordinary validated command, regardless of human or AI controller.

Invariant: each initial God exists at exactly one place; there is no off-map abstraction during migration, hibernation or burial. Its owner always knows its anchor, orders and condition through the control interface, but that does not reveal unseen surrounding terrain or foreign units. Foreign observers may lose its location under fog or underground. The God remains selectable from the persistent God dock whenever alive, including while asleep or immobilized.

### 4.2 Compositional generation grammar

A genome is a validated composition, not independent random picks:

`family -> compatible locomotor -> feasible habitat -> compatible feeding apparatus/diet -> sensory organ -> compatible tail -> optional armor -> optional ecological organ -> optional symbiosis -> size/palette/temperament -> spatial viability proof`.

| Family | Structural identity | Legal primary locomotors | Civic pressure |
|---|---|---|---|
| Q: Pillarback | Continuous arched torso, four articulated limbs, forward feeding head | PILLAR, WEBBED, DIGGER | Broad terrestrial footprint; supports Godways, sanctuaries and agricultural conflict |
| H: Burden Crawler | Low segmented body, six articulated limbs, distinct anterior feeding module | PILLAR, DIGGER | Mining and ground stability; slow but powerful, many ground contacts |
| S: Ribbon | Continuous long axial body, no walking limbs; flexible body trail | UNDULATOR, FIN | Narrow migration corridors, rivers and burrowing access; long vulnerable supply frontier |
| M: Mantle | Broad low central mantle, paired lateral membranes, ventral feeding opening | FIN, WING | Coastal/river economies or landing-site networks; wide body, fragile membranes |

These are nine legal family/primary-locomotor pairings, not nine complete authored creatures. No terrestrial legs are pasted onto a Mantle; no extra arms or humanoid face are allowed. Body family stays fixed for V1, while locomotion and structures can change under Section 5. A family alone does not determine diet, temperament, institution or military role.

Feeding apparatus is BROWSE, FILTER, MINERAL or CARRION. FILTER requires shallow/deep-water access with FIN or WEBBED; MINERAL requires suitable exposed ore or a legal burrowing configuration; BROWSE requires reachable biomass and a surface feeding stance; CARRION consumes wildlife or prepared food, not citizens. M accepts FILTER or CARRION; Q accepts BROWSE, MINERAL or CARRION plus FILTER only when WEBBED; H accepts BROWSE, MINERAL or CARRION; S accepts all four subject to habitat. Photosynthesis is a supplement, never free complete upkeep.

Sensory choices are EYE-RING, ANTENNA-CROWN and VIBRATION-ROSETTE. Tails are BALANCE, PADDLE and WEDGE, with NONE allowed for H. Q accepts BALANCE/PADDLE; H accepts NONE/WEDGE; S accepts all three; M accepts PADDLE/BALANCE. Armor is NONE, OSTEODERM, SHELL or COAT; WING forbids SHELL and OSTEODERM. One ecological organ slot accepts NONE, GILLS, SOLAR-SAIL, THERMAL-VENT or SPORE-SAC. One symbiosis slot accepts NONE, FUNGAL-GROVE, FOLLOWER-GALLERY or ROOT-HOLDFAST. WING forbids galleries/holdfast; DIGGER forbids exposed sails/groves; illegal combinations are rejected, not cosmetically hidden.

Temperament has three integer axes: stimulation tolerance, territorial attachment and follower affinity, each 0..1000. These affect costs, stress and institutional responses, never ownership or fundamental obedience. Palettes follow approved material families and habitat-biased bounded values; biome color alone never counts as a different God.

A generated recipe must produce a capability vector containing navigation domains, footprint pattern, diet channels, rest sites, environmental impacts, vulnerabilities and civic services. Pairwise variation tests require differences in at least four of those seven dimensions between offered start previews. Validate all allowed module pairs and 200 seeded complete recipes; the raw Cartesian product is not a promised count of meaningful creatures.

### 4.3 Size and footprint representation

Size I/II/III has mass multiplier 1/2/4, base upkeep 8/16/32 nutrition, reserve capacity 48/96/192, and primary health capacity 800/1200/1600. Health is represented per tissue region plus a vital pool; damage totals cannot be inferred from visual size alone. Body age and size are separate: an old God may remain physically small.

Q uses two-cell, three-cell triangular and seven-cell disc footprints. H uses three-cell triangle, four-cell rhombus and seven-cell disc. S uses a three-, five- or seven-cell ordered body trail, advancing its head and releasing its tail; it can bend around a river. M uses three-cell fan, five-cell fan and seven-cell disc at rest; airborne wings use the same projected footprint in the air domain, never surface occupation while flying. All offsets rotate in six hex headings; the exact canonical masks and wrap fixtures are specified in Section 16.

A move validates the swept footprint, occupancy domain, turning clearance, slope, channel width, bridge/Godway support and consent boundaries. The mesh pose follows the authoritative trail/mask; visual IK cannot decide whether a route is legal. Corridors for a colossal God are consequently not equivalent to corridors for an army.

### 4.4 Direct interaction contract

Click/tap the God, its dock or its outliner row; `G` focuses it without cancelling its order. Selecting it exposes a persistent action strip and on-map destination/impact preview. Right-click issues a safe move on desktop; touch uses select -> destination -> confirm. `Shift` appends at most twelve waypoints. `Escape` cancels a draft/target, not a committed historical action. Enter-turn is disabled only for unresolved mandatory choices, with an explicit override for harmless idle actors.

The God control state machine is `NONE -> SELECTED -> TARGETING -> PREVIEW -> QUEUED`; resolution then produces `MOVING`, `ACTING`, `RESTING`, `HIBERNATING`, `MOLTING`, `IMMOBILIZED` or `DEAD`. This is strategic direct control, not WASD possession. The player chooses route, stance, action target and development. Default command latency is zero planning turns: a legal queued order executes in the next resolution. Long tasks display progress and interruption cost.

Every preview distinguishes certain own-state costs from estimated/unknown consequences under fog. It must not reveal hidden enemy units through path costs or collision previews. A route into unexplored terrain stops at a newly discovered obstacle rather than retroactively allowing an impossible move. Emergency warnings identify both a safe alternative and the consequences of overriding safety where physically possible.

### 4.5 Closed God command set

| Command | Cost and duration | Spatial/action result | Failure and override policy |
|---|---|---|---|
| MOVE | Terrain AP; normally 1 per cell edge, 2 in difficult terrain | Advance validated footprint/trail; consume 1 extra nutrition per AP spent | Stop before unconsented damage, unknown obstruction or illegal domain |
| FEED | 2 AP; one target feeding zone or delivered stock | Take up to two turns of base upkeep, limited by actual stock/capacity | Never conjure nutrition; protected/foreign extraction needs rights or explicit incident consent |
| REST | Whole remaining turn; no attack | Fatigue -30; regenerate eligible tissues; occupy resting footprint | The player can interrupt next planning phase |
| HIBERNATE | Choose 4, 8 or 12 turns; upkeep 25% | Immobile sanctuary; fatigue recovers; specific adaptations progress | Wake order takes one turn, adds 25 fatigue and consumes one normal upkeep; never a random refusal |
| MIGRATE | Twelve-waypoint route plus optional feeding/rest stops | Repeats safe MOVE/FEED/REST using the player's explicit itinerary | Any new treaty violation, severe injury or support shortfall stops and requests orders |
| GUARD | 2 AP stance; persists | Deter passage, protect adjacent supply and declared settlement; no automatic God escalation | Reaction strikes target armies only if rules of engagement allow; God target requires explicit consent |
| STRIKE | 2 AP, +20 fatigue, +one base upkeep nutrition; maximum one strike per impulse | Resolve visible target and footprint collateral through common combat/impact rules | Preview known casualties, war status and escape route; command invalid if cost cannot be paid |
| SHAPE | 4 AP, +25 fatigue, +two base upkeep nutrition | Use installed structure capability to excavate, dredge, brace, seed or vent in one cell | Exact legal effects in capability table; no arbitrary terrain painting |
| CULTIVATE | 2 AP plus civic supplies; 2-4 turns | Start eligible Godform change at chosen viable site | Physical module change occurs atomically on completion; cancellation returns no spent supplies |
| ASSIST | 2 AP and one base upkeep extra | Supply one permitted local construction, protection, transport or ecological service | Service is recorded as divine-dependent; no global production buff |
| HOLD | Zero AP | Stay, use configured ration reserve; no autonomous travel | Shortage and threat alerts remain active |
| WITHDRAW | Same route costs as MOVE; defensive stance | Follow a chosen legal retreat path, forgoing attacks | No teleport, invulnerability or automatic safe destination |

Stances are CAREFUL, NORMAL and FORCEFUL. CAREFUL adds one AP to the first move that turn and halves disturbance; NORMAL uses base costs; FORCEFUL removes that careful surcharge and allows already-confirmed destructive passage, adds 15 fatigue and increases disturbance by 50%. FORCEFUL is not permission to start God combat or kill civilians without the corresponding explicit action consent. Terrain/health impossibility cannot be overridden by attitude.

### 4.6 Needs, feeding, rest and recovery

Nutrition comes from reachable actual sources: plant biomass for BROWSE; aquatic biomass/water quality for FILTER; mineral reserve/ORE for MINERAL; wildlife or FOOD for CARRION. FOOD delivery can replace only an explicitly compatible fraction of diet: BROWSE 50%, FILTER 25%, MINERAL 0%, CARRION 100%. MEDICINE and TOOLS are treatment materials, not food. Photosynthetic organs supply at most 30% of base upkeep while exposed in suitable light/moisture; they do not refill reserves while burrowed or hibernating.

Terrain ecological pools are in 0..1000 biomass units; ten pool units equal one nutrition. Regeneration is `floor((capacity - current) * rate / 1000)` with minimum one only when below capacity and conditions permit. Rates are 60 for grass, 30 for woodland, 80 for wetland/shallow water, 15 for tundra/desert and 0 for exhausted minerals. Each source retains extraction and contamination history. Civic farms use their own yields but compete for the same land, moisture and contamination.

At turn-end, deduct base upkeep plus action extras from reserves/delivered rations. A shortfall raises fatigue by 15 and damages vital health by `20 * sizeMass` per turn. Zero reserves does not make the God wander or eat cities; the player chooses a response. Always show a two-turn shortage warning when own information makes it predictable. Prepared feeding schedules may run automatically only within named rights, reserve thresholds and protected-area limits.

Fatigue is 0..100. At 60+, movement budget drops to three AP; at 85+, two AP; at 100, only REST, FEED, HOLD or a one-edge emergency retreat are legal. REST heals 10 vital health and 20 regional health per turn when fed; an infirmary with medicine doubles regional healing. Severe wounds need a matching treatment action and several turns; rest does not instantly regrow a severed structure. Waking, molting and heavy injury have explicit costs and visible posture changes.

### 4.7 Injury, age and relationship

Regions are vital core, locomotor structures, feeding apparatus, sensory structures and defensive structures. Wounds have type (bruise, tear, fracture, infection, lost structure), severity 1..3, region and cause. A locomotor fracture removes one AP and changes gait; a damaged filter reduces food conversion; sensory injury reduces observation radius; cracked shell reduces protection. Loss of a required structure disables its capability until treated or replaced through an eligible development, and its geometry must show that state.

Regrowth is permitted only for membranes and sensory fronds, requiring four fed rest turns and medicine. Major severed limbs do not regrow in V1. A God with insufficient supporting limbs becomes immobile but remains controllable for feeding, treatment, rest and policy decisions; civil engineers can construct support cradles through the ASSIST/structure system, not a new limb model.

Age increments every turn. Young is 0-59 turns, mature 60-159, ancient 160+. Ancient Gods acquire one chronic integrity loss of 5 vital health per turn unless their installed/maintained adaptations and medical service provide 5 or more longevity support. Longevity support is capped at 5; it arrests this age loss, not injuries or starvation. The UI warns twenty turns before the threshold. There is no unannounced random death roll.

Follower trust, welfare and perceived burden are tracked at 0..1000. Treatment, equitable provisioning and safe routes raise trust; injuries, extraction and collateral damage affect public burden and factions. Low trust raises institutional costs and makes some civic services inefficient; it never allows the God AI to confiscate player control. Affinity changes the cost of gallery/pilgrimage services, not whether a valid move command is obeyed.

## 5. GODFORM: historical development, not a talent tree

Development is an evidence-and-construction process. Every turn records bounded exposures: wet habitat, cold, heat, mineral diet, migration distance, followers served, injuries endured, time resting and institutional investment. Each exposure is an integer 0..1000, updated as a 20-turn exponentially weighted history: `new = floor((19*old + thisTurn)/20)`. Lifetime milestones are separate monotonic counters.

An eligible recipe appears when its ecological evidence, civic knowledge, anatomy and physical site conditions hold. At most three eligible recipes are presented at a decision event, alongside maintaining the current body. The player may inspect all recipes and deliberately cultivate the conditions for one. There are no abstract experience points or generic ability-point purchases. Offers recur at most once every ten turns unless a newly severe wound creates a relevant treatment opportunity.

At most three major adaptations may be active, counting armor/ecological organ/symbiosis and transformed locomotion beyond the starting baseline. The starting feeding and sensory modules do not spend this limit. A development can replace a slot; removal has a visible recovery period and resource cost. At most two locomotor conversions are allowed over a life, recorded permanently. Family changes are excluded. Size growth is optional, separate from the three slots, and irreversible: I -> II requires age at least 20, twenty well-fed turns and 40 FOOD/20 TOOLS; II -> III requires age at least 100, forty additional well-fed turns and 80 FOOD/40 TOOLS. Mineral feeders pay ORE instead of FOOD at the same quantity. Growth takes four rest turns and expands the validated footprint; it cannot complete without room.

## 6. Civilization simulation and two-way dependency

### 6.1 Population, settlements and portable society

One population unit represents 1,000 people; store thousandths of a population unit to avoid disappearance through rounding. Settlement population is capped at 32 units, world settlements at 96, each civilization at 16. Cities contain an administrative core and up to six functional district parcels, with housing clusters derived from population. Adjacent worked cells may contain farms, extraction and infrastructure. The cap is a declared V1 map/readability limit; UI explains it before a command is issued.

Households consume 2 FOOD per population per turn. Four service dimensions are food security, health, housing and safety, each 0..1000; welfare is their equally weighted mean. Growth at welfare >=700 is `min(0.10 population, population*0.01)` per turn, limited by housing and two-turn food reserve. Welfare below 350 produces emigration pressure up to 0.10 population/turn; famine deaths begin only after two shortage turns, after opportunities to evacuate. Population, cargo and refugees are conserved except explicit birth/death events.

A settlement of at most eight population can pack into a portable settlement over two turns. It consumes 30 TIMBER/10 TOOLS, leaves persistent foundations, and moves as a caravan with actual population and inventory. Unpacking takes two turns on a legal site. Towns above eight must first relocate people; they cannot teleport a metropolis. A gallery-equipped God can carry exactly one such settlement, but passengers still consume food and need a legal evacuation plan. Without galleries it moves on the same road/shipping network in wagon groups. Portable settlements may trade, govern and research at half stationary output; they cannot extract from a moving location. Permanent waystations and port agreements can therefore support a genuinely mobile trade civilization.

### 6.2 Economy and accounting

The nine quantities are FOOD, TIMBER, STONE, ORE, TOOLS, MEDICINE, BIO, COIN and KNOWLEDGE. The first seven are locally stored physical cargo. COIN is a civilization ledger with trade escrow; KNOWLEDGE is research progress generated by staffed civic institutions. Neither is magically delivered physical cargo. BIO is harvested biological substrate from sustainable God services, authorized exploitation or remains; it is optional for ordinary civic survival. Numeric tables display whole resource units; typed storage multiplies by 100.

All quantities use integer hundredths, a common `Quantity` type, bounded to safe integer arithmetic. No floating stock subtraction. Sites have at most four staffed jobs. Seventy-five percent of population is available as job labor; the remaining quarter covers domestic care and basic administration. Labor uses milli-worker units as specified in Section 16.5. Default governor allocation is food to two-turn reserve, then current construction inputs, then research/trade, with player locks taking precedence.

Base settlement storage per physical resource is 40; granary adds 160 FOOD, depot adds 100 to each other physical resource. Overflow spoils at 10%/turn after capacity, with a warning and export opportunity. Base settlement yield is 2 FOOD and 1 KNOWLEDGE even before specialization; local dues yield 1 COIN per full population unit when household food coverage is at least 500/1000. These do not scale with God condition. Basic HALL administration is supplied by the non-working quarter of population; its COIN upkeep remains real. Base construction labor is four work per staffed builder per turn, with fractional labor carried exactly. `PRODUCTION` is work applied to a queue, not a tenth stockpile.

The starting inventory is 60 FOOD, 40 TIMBER, 30 STONE, 10 ORE, 10 TOOLS, 4 MEDICINE, 0 BIO and 40 COIN; KNOWLEDGE starts at zero. The God starts with three base-upkeep turns of reserves. Initial five population can work two farms (8 FOOD) plus base 2 FOOD to meet household consumption before God's wild feeding. The start validator must additionally provide sustainable wild support; this arithmetic is not permission to start a God in barren terrain.

### 6.3 Construction, cities and infrastructure

Buildings use fourteen functions: DWELLING, HALL, MARKET, ARCHIVE, WORKSHOP, GRANARY, INFIRMARY, SHRINE, OBSERVATORY, BARRACKS, TOWER, DOCK, DEPOT and CARAVAN-CANOPY. A district can contain one major function plus housing/decorative clusters. Housing grows at population thresholds 2/5/9/15/23, using derived clusters rather than five independently authored city models.

Base minor building cost is 12 TIMBER/8 STONE/10 work. HALL, DOCK and OBSERVATORY cost twice this; CARAVAN-CANOPY costs 8 TIMBER/4 TOOLS/6 work. Repair costs `ceil(originalCost * damageFraction * 0.6)` in each input plus the same fraction of work. Upkeep is 1 COIN per staffed building per turn; HALL/DOCK/OBSERVATORY cost 2. Unstaffed buildings retain housing/geometry but provide no active services. One HALL per settlement is mandatory and included at founding.

Infrastructure families are ROAD, BRIDGE, FARM, MINE, CANAL and WALL. Forestry/quarry/fishery are modes of FARM/MINE/DOCK assemblies with explicit finite variants in the asset catalog. Base edge/site construction costs are ROAD 3 TIMBER/3 work, BRIDGE 8 TIMBER/4 STONE/8 work, FARM 4 TIMBER/4 work, MINE 8 TIMBER/4 TOOLS/8 work, CANAL 10 STONE/5 TOOLS/12 work, WALL 8 STONE/6 work. Godway upgrade multiplies road/bridge material and work costs by three. Maintenance is one material of the dominant construction type per four active segments/turn, rounded up locally.

Ordinary roads reduce army/cargo AP cost, not a God's ecological footprint. Godways increase carrying capacity and reduce trample damage by 75%. Canals expand width-1 river links to width 2 subject to elevation, but cannot cut through bedrock or create an ocean. Ports require a live water connection: silt or subsidence can disable them and force relocation. The same site cannot be both a productive farm and an occupied resting body parcel.

Three civic megaprojects reuse approved building/infrastructure modules: EXCHANGE (HALL+MARKET+DOCK), WORLD-OBSERVATORY (ARCHIVE+OBSERVATORY+TOWER), GREAT-WEIR (CANAL+BRIDGE+DEPOT). Each costs 100 STONE/60 TOOLS/120 work, needs the relevant tier-4 technology and a valid connected site. They supply, respectively, network capacity, public historical/research access and flood/food resilience. No bespoke wonder illustration or mesh is hidden in this scope.

### 6.5 Logistics and trade

A supply network is a capacity graph of settlement/depot/port nodes and road/river/coastal edges. Physical consignments are debited at dispatch, travel a legal route and credit at arrival. A convoy carries at most twenty resource units, takes at least one turn, and can be intercepted, halted or redirected. Civilizations may own up to twenty active convoys; world cap is 128 including refugee transports. Congestion causes queues, not invisible instant delivery.

Army supply is drawn locally or delivered; distance alone is not a resource. God provisions require the actual last-mile feeding zone, not simply being inside national borders. Network flow allocates in player priority order: households, committed treatment, army rations, God delivery, construction, exports. Priorities are editable with explicit starvation consequences. A treaty cannot spend another civilization's stock. Trade prices are explicit agreed exchange rates/COIN, with escrow on dispatch and settlement on delivery; seized goods and failed deliveries are accounted events.

### 7.3 Emergent social identities

Five institutional blocs are Stewards, Extractors, Devotional Houses, Civic Assemblies and Security Orders. Each settlement tracks support shares summing to 1000. Events, employment, policy and visible benefits/costs update support by at most 20 points per turn, redistributed proportionally from other blocs. Support does not teleport from one extreme to another.

Worship emerges from repeated visible protection, healing, pilgrimage employment and divine-charter policy. Scientific study emerges from archives, observatories and sustained observations. Exploitation requires physical worksites and a supported extraction policy. Containment emerges from God-free zoning, braced borders and anti-incursion treaties. Rejection emerges from accumulated casualties, service substitutes and anti-divine zoning; it can mean protecting cities from Gods, not compulsory execution. Secular bureaucracy emerges from archives, civic charter and redundant services. Divine-right government is the combination of divine charter, concentrated services and devotional majority; militarized deterrence requires actual escorts/defense and security support.

These are derived descriptive states with transparent causes, not starting civilization traits. An anti-divine society can still protect a dying God, or trade in remains; no automatic ideology script overrides player decisions. Stable disagreement is possible. Low legitimacy below 300 for three turns creates strikes (one fewer staffed job per affected city), tax loss and emigration. At below 150 for five turns, a city may demand autonomy; the player can negotiate, reform or occupy. V1 autonomous cities remain neutral treaty actors using existing assets and population, but do not gain a new God or become additional full AI civilizations.

### 7.4 Dependency ledger

For food support, production assistance, transport, protection, medicine and legitimacy, record delivered service units with source class `CIVIC`, `DIVINE`, `TRADE` and replacement lead time. Essential service dependency is the rolling 20-turn divine share, weighted by population served. Show an explicit God-unavailable forecast for 4/12/20 turns using present stock and known routes. This is a forecast, not a guaranteed future world.

When a God sleeps, migrates or dies, only its actual services cease. Their local customers experience measured gaps; unrelated distant cities do not receive a universal debuff. A God-independent society has constructed or traded for replacement services. Independence is costly and useful rather than a menu checkbox.

## 8. Warfare, deterrence and catastrophe

### 8.1 Armies and fleets

An army contains up to six companies, each 0.20 population when recruited. Company types are MILITIA, INFANTRY, ARCHER, ENGINEER and SIEGE. Each has strength, cohesion 0..100, equipment condition and rations. Recruitment costs respectively 8/12/12/10/20 FOOD plus 0/4/4/6/10 TOOLS and 4/6/6/6/10 work. Troops remain linked to origin cohorts for casualties and demobilization. Unit visuals use the approved soldier/civilian/loadout assemblies, not five unrelated character sets.

Base strengths are 8/14/10/6/8; SIEGE has structure power 30 instead of 8. INFANTRY carries spear/shield; ARCHER uses bow; ENGINEER uses a civilian-and-work-wagon assembly; SIEGE uses the ballista plus crew. Armor modifies damage, not binary immunity. One army model group represents its companies; there is no click-per-soldier control.

Fleets use MERCHANT and WAR hulls. Merchant hull capacity is twenty cargo or two companies; war hull carries one company and protects/intercepts. Hulls require ports, FOOD/TOOLS upkeep and actual coastal/deep routes; open-sea passage unlocks at T-NET-3. Naval combat uses the same cohesion/supply model. No free embarkation or teleporting ships between basins.

### 8.2 Combat and logistics

An army consumes one FOOD per company per turn and one TOOLS per two supplied combat turns, rounded up. Two turns without rations reduce cohesion by 15/turn and prevent siege progress. Terrain multiplies effective strength: open 1000, defensive hills 1200, woodland defense 1150, river crossing attack 750, urban defense 1250, all in permille. Morale and equipment apply bounded 500..1000 factors.

For ordinary combat, each impulse inflicts cohesion damage `clamp(floor(20 * attackPower / max(1, defensePower)), 2, 35)` simultaneously. Lost cohesion produces casualties equal to damage/1000 population per company engaged, capped by company population. Cohesion <=20 triggers the configured retreat route if legal; otherwise surrender after another impulse. Casualties, prisoners and demobilization never duplicate population. Random tactical critical hits are excluded; fog, simultaneous intentions and strategic positioning provide uncertainty.

Walls start at 300 integrity, towers 150 and major buildings 100. Siege damage first reduces fortification; occupation requires an army ending a turn with supply in an undefended core. Occupation does not instantly annex culture or erase population: contested administration lasts ten turns, with welfare/treaty consequences. A God may breach walls but cannot govern or occupy a city by itself.

### 8.3 God participation and anti-God defense

A God strike deals 50 cohesion damage to ordinary companies in its targeted occupied cell, modified by preparation and shelter, and 80 structure integrity damage. Against another God it deals `120 * sizeMass / targetSizeMass` regional/vital damage before armor, with a floor of 30. Attacker pays the real fatigue/reserve cost in Section 4.5. Defensive OSTEODERM/SHELL reduces physical damage by 25%; no stack to invulnerability. Limb targeting must use observed anatomy; no perfect vital targeting through fog.

Prepared SIEGE/INFANTRY in a braced T-ENG-3 emplacement can inflict 20/8 regional God damage per supplied company per impulse, respectively. Unprepared companies inflict one-quarter of this. Attrition, denied feeding access, damaged limbs and blocked retreat can defeat a God; a handful of ordinary militia cannot casually erase it. Poisoned food sabotage and hidden instant-kill espionage are excluded from V1.

God retreat obeys footprint/path/supply rules. A crippled God may need several strategic turns of treatment; presentation remains brief and skippable, and no cinematic grants immunity. Armies matter through screening, evacuation, chokepoints, siege and civic occupation even when Gods are present.

### 8.4 Rare God encounters

Diplomatic proximity warnings appear when observed reachable footprints could intersect within two turns. The AI assigns high expected loss to a God encounter, not a hidden prohibition. Gods can meet peacefully, block one another or negotiate passage. War does not automatically mean every nearby God attacks.

An explicit God strike on another God creates an encounter object linking the two actors, nearby affected cells and causes; it does not change simulation mode. Each strike applies impact intensity `40 * attackerSizeMass` to occupied cells and half to adjacent cells, damages infrastructure, lowers biomass, adds contamination and creates civilian flight pressure. Elevation/drainage effects obey the same bounded spatial operations. Neutral parties receive observed incidents, not omniscient alerts with hidden coordinates.

AI escalation is legal only when expected civic loss from inaction exceeds the estimated encounter loss, or a survival/capital-defense objective has no safer viable action. The estimate includes its own God death, food network breakage, population displacement and treaty response. Normal-difficulty unattended 240-turn campaigns should have a median of zero or one direct God clash, without scripted invulnerability or a hard encounter quota. Failure of this distribution is a balance/AI defect to examine, not a number to fake.

### 8.5 Civilians, ruins and recovery

At a warned danger zone, evacuation orders move real population/cargo toward capacity-limited shelters. Destruction adds displacement pressure before casualty resolution. Sheltered population has 75% reduced collateral deaths; unsheltered casualties are capped by actual present cohorts and recorded. Refugees occupy transport capacity, need food and retain origin history. No decorative fleeing crowd substitutes for this accounting.

Destroyed buildings remain as derived ruins; roads/walls retain damaged segments; rebuilding consumes local materials/work. Reconstructing a district preserves its site history and can deliberately reuse corpse structures. Recovery is an economic and diplomatic phase, not a global reset when the battle animation ends.

## 9. Diplomacy, information and access

Diplomatic state is contact, trust (-1000..1000), observed incidents, active proposals, accepted clauses, claims, war and relationship history. Treaties are typed enforceable records with parties, scope cells/edges, terms, start/end turns, compensation, termination notice and cause IDs. Different clauses may cover the same place only if their rights are compatible; validator rejects contradictions.

| Clause ID | Meaning and enforcement |
|---|---|
| D-BORDER | Recognize ownership claims in enumerated cells; no automatic God access |
| D-TRADE | Goods/rates, route permission, escrow and tariff terms |
| D-ALLIANCE | Defensive assistance obligation, declared response window and actual feasible forces |
| D-PASSAGE | Ordinary armies/civilians transit named cells without annexation |
| D-GOD-CORRIDOR | God-specific body size/domain and direction, access window, maintained clearance |
| D-FEEDING | Diet/source quotas, protected stocks, restitution for excess |
| D-SANCTUARY | No offensive action in named rest/treatment cells, evacuation rights |
| D-SACRED-SITE | Pilgrimage/visitor/excavation permissions independent of political ownership |
| D-RESTITUTION | Actual goods/COIN delivery schedule for recorded infrastructure damage |
| D-DETERRENCE | Declared warning buffers and observed-force notifications; not global surveillance |
| D-REMAINS | Corpse-layer rights: territory, excavation, preservation and visitor access |
| D-RESEARCH | Share named observation/study results, not unrestricted true state |
| D-ASYLUM | Admit real displaced cohorts subject to housing/food capacity |
| D-NONAGGRESSION | Prohibit initiation of combat for fixed duration with notice |
| D-ENCOUNTER-AVOIDANCE | Maintain God separation buffer and alternating corridor schedules |
| D-COMPACT | End-state multilateral charter with ratified subordinate obligations |

A safe God move respects these permissions. The player may choose breach with a preview of known costs; the action is not secretly cancelled because a neighbor dislikes it. An incident records direct damage, observers, disputed responsibility and restitution. Foreign belief can be incomplete; the omniscient engine record is not automatically shared with every faction.

V1 intelligence consists of scouts, trade observations, local tracks, observation towers and research exchange. No separate spy-agent unit, espionage minigame or omniscient diplomatic valuation exists. A last-seen God track has position, time, visible traits and a reachable uncertainty region expanded with known movement capability. Unknown diet/health is a range. AI and player access the same observation API. Save/debug tools may expose full state only outside normal gameplay and must be visibly marked.

## 10. Permanent death and post-God civilization

### 10.1 Transition transaction

When vital health reaches zero, resolve death exactly once. Cancel/refund only unspent orders/reservations, evacuate or resolve actual gallery passengers, terminate live-body services, retain owner identity and create a corpse at the final body footprint/trail. The body is not removed for camera convenience. The owner remains a normal civilization with all territory, people, armies, research and legal claims. A persistent memorial dock replaces unavailable living actions and links to the corpse, service-gap forecast and history.

If death occurs in air, find the deterministic downwind/swept projected landing footprint among the current and six adjacent cells, prioritizing current projection; apply collapse damage and record the path. It may be catastrophic but must not teleport to a convenient empty tile. A sea death sinks at its location into the seabed navigation/claim layer and leaves a visible surface remains marker; access later requires a port/research route. A burrowed death remains underground with subsidence/evidence at its real cells. Corpse rendering uses the same actual morphology, injuries and scale as the living God.

### 10.2 Finite remains and succession

At death create tissue reserve `100 * sizeMass`, mineral reserve `60 * sizeMass`, contamination potential `40 * sizeMass` and a persistent structural skeleton/carapace graph. Reserve units are resource quantities, not population. The exact shape is derived from the actual family and installed body modules; there is no generic humanoid skeleton.

| Stage | Baseline age since death | Ecology and exploitation | Visual rule |
|---|---|---|---|
| RECENT | 0-3 turns / years [0,20) | Highest contamination risk; evacuation/preservation; BIO harvesting possible | Actual fallen body, injuries retained, lowered saturation, no breathing |
| DECAY | 4-23 turns / years [20,120) | Tissue extraction, decomposer bloom, downstream water hazards | Shrinking tissue coverage; approved fungal growth/rubble; visible exposed structure |
| OSSUARY | 24-79 turns / years [120,400) | Remaining bone/mineral extraction, fortress/necropolis construction, scientific study | Family-specific rib/carapace/axial framework; buildable negative spaces |
| FOSSIL | 80+ turns / 400+ years | Finite ore/heritage studies; permanent terrain/ownership consequences | Mineralized remnants, weathered surface and inherited settlement foundations |

Cold/dry preservation doubles stage durations; warm/wet exposure multiplies them by 3/4, rounded up. Extraction advances effective decay by one additional turn per five BIO taken. Preserving a site consumes 2 MEDICINE and 2 COIN per turn and halves decay advance but never reverses it. Depleted tissue does not regenerate; depleted minerals cannot be mined again. Complete excavation removes extracted structural pieces, leaving landforms, foundations and historical markers. No stage returns life or spawns a replacement God.

### 10.3 Post-God institutions and agency

The player may preserve, study, open pilgrimage, selectively mine, fortify or build around remains using existing policies/buildings. These can coexist by spatial parcel and treaty rights, not a single binary corpse toggle. Foreign civilizations can request/reject research, visitor and excavation rights, claim disputed cells, send actual expeditions and fight over access. A city's economic boom depends on real visitors/resources; a collapse depends on its actual lost service share.

Death creates a one-time historical shock scaled to local dependency and relationship, decaying over twenty turns. A divine-charter center may suffer severe legitimacy loss; a redundant secular city may experience grief and new politics without economic collapse. Faction support shifts remain bounded per turn. The user always has concrete decisions: ration replacement, evacuation, industrial retooling, rights negotiation and memorial investment. The campaign must support a God dying before turn 30 and a viable civilization continuing to turn 240 without a hidden rescue buff.

## 11. AI architecture and parity

No generative model is in the runtime. The AI uses deterministic utility scoring, bounded candidate planning and the same command validator as the human.

**Observation layer:** constructs a civilization-specific immutable `Observation` from visible cells, own actors, legally shared data and last-seen estimates. AI modules receive no raw world-state reference. A test proxy throws if an unobserved field is queried. Debug knowledge is in a separate build path.

**Strategic director:** every five turns or after a major event chooses up to three goals from secure food, stabilize welfare, expand, connect trade, develop God, reduce dependency, deter, wage war, protect remains and pursue an ending. It uses a 20-turn coarse forecast of its own economy and observed threats, not a full omniscient world search.

**Economy governor:** each turn solves local job/queue/resource priorities greedily with reserved resources and a two-turn survival floor. It may not spend the same resource in two plans. **Settlement planner:** scores at most 24 reachable sites by water/food, transport, footprint conflict, claims and strategic value. **Diplomacy planner:** evaluates typed offers and schedules; it refuses impossible supplies or clearance. **War planner:** assigns supported armies to finite objectives and validates retreat/supply. **God controller:** enumerates at most 24 legal macro-actions including hold, feed, rest, route segment, guard, assist, retreat and eligible development.

All candidates use normalized 0..1000 terms. Base score is `4*survival + 3*civicWelfare + 2*economicGain + 2*goalProgress + informationValue - 4*irreversibleRisk - 3*diplomaticCost - 2*dependencyGrowth - actionCost`. Personality changes weights by at most 25%, never rules. God-death expected cost includes the lost trained body and all dependent service gaps. The risk assessor considers ecology, fatigue, enemy uncertainty and escape footprint. It prefers information gathering or a robust safe route when uncertainty is large, not pretending a probability estimate is fact.

Before accepting a plan, run a reservation reconciliation and final validation. If every action is invalid, issue a legal hold/rest and record the reason. There is no random teleport, free food, infinite research or immunity to decline. At difficulty levels, change search breadth (8/16/24 God candidates and 8/16/24 sites), forecast horizon (8/12/20 turns) and error tolerance only. No hidden numerical bonuses in V1.

Compute budgets are operation-count limits for deterministic decisions, never wall-clock cutoffs that change results across machines. Wall-clock profiling may identify a performance defect, but does not alter which AI candidate wins in a replay. Ties use stable IDs and a seeded personality stream. Long-campaign reports record starvation, idle-invalid orders, treaty breaches, God survival, resilience and victory progress, rather than judging competence from one screenshot.

## 12. Camera, UI and information hierarchy

### 12.1 Camera and input

An orthographic camera defaults to azimuth 45 degrees and elevation 55 degrees, with rotation in smooth 60-degree steps and tilt limited to 35-70 degrees. Zoom spans 4-40 visible hex widths, automatically choosing semantic LOD. The camera does not zoom through the God body; focusing uses its complete bounds and preserves a minimum readable city scale. Near/far planes are fitted to map height and view, not enormous hardcoded extremes.

Mouse drag or WASD pans; wheel/pinch zooms; Q/E rotates; G focuses God; C cycles cities; A cycles armies; Tab cycles actionable objects; Escape backs out; Space opens the turn summary but does not silently commit. Enter confirms the explicit end-turn control. Rebindings, disabled edge scroll by default and browser focus guards are mandatory. No pointer lock or gamepad requirement in V1.

Touch uses one-finger pan on empty map, tap selection, two-finger pinch/rotate and a dedicated confirm button. A drag threshold separates movement from taps. No critical feature depends on hover/right-click. At <=900 CSS px, sidebars become one bottom sheet with a pinned God dock and end-turn control. Portrait tablet retains at least 55% map height outside modal management screens. Minimum targets are 44 CSS px; body text defaults to 16 px and supports 200% browser zoom.

Alerts are actionable links, not decorative exclamation marks.

### 12.3 Map readability

Overlays are exclusive base modes with at most two secondary layers: ownership, food/ecology, logistics, God footprint/forecast, diplomacy/access, geology/stability, dependency and history. Selected footprint boundaries remain visible through partial body occlusion. A configurable cutaway fades only the selected occluding presentation mesh; it does not remove collision or disclose hidden actors. Terrain uses restrained colors; faction identity appears in borders, banners and accents, not a neon-colored landscape.

At close zoom show buildings, cargo and company groups. At middle zoom simplify people/vegetation and retain city/God silhouette. At far zoom use derived emblems/badges, route lines and labeled settlements; living Gods retain a low-detail 3D body to communicate geography. Critical symbols pair color with shape/text. Reduced-motion mode disables camera shake, flashing and continuous idle movement while retaining state transitions and route visibility.

### 12.4 Notifications and historical explanation

Alert severity is BLOCKING (illegal/required choice), DANGER (two-turn predicted shortage, imminent encounter), CHANGE (completed construction/evolution/route revision) or HISTORY (recorded event). Identical alerts coalesce by cause and location. The turn review highlights at most five highest-impact changes plus an expandable log. Every numerical modifier has a provenance breakdown; forecasts expose uncertainty and relevant observation age.

## 13. History, saves and reproducibility

### 13.1 Event architecture

Commands produce immutable semantic events with `eventId, turn, impulse, type, actorIds, locationIds, causeIds, observerCivIds, payload, schemaVersion`. IDs are monotonically allocated within a campaign seed namespace, not wall-clock UUIDs in simulation. Cause edges form a directed acyclic graph by event sequence. Events can cite earlier treaties, construction or injuries, allowing a harbor's relocation to be traced to a migration or corpse deposit.

The closed high-level event types are CITY_FOUNDED, CITY_PACKED, CITY_UNPACKED, CITY_DESTROYED, ROUTE_CHANGED, TERRITORY_CHANGED, TRADE_INCIDENT, TREATY_SIGNED, TREATY_BREACHED, WAR_STARTED, WAR_ENDED, GOD_MIGRATED, GOD_INJURED, GOD_RECOVERED, GOD_EVOLVED, GOD_ENCOUNTER, GOD_DIED, REMAINS_TRANSFORMED, REMAINS_EXTRACTED, POPULATION_DISPLACED, INSTITUTION_SHIFTED, DISASTER, MEGAPROJECT_FINISHED and ENDING_REACHED. Routine stock deltas remain in turn summaries; they do not each become a narrative headline.

History prose uses deterministic authored text templates in code/data, not runtime AI. Locations retain original and later names and a chain of causes. Event visibility is civilization-specific; post-game omniscient inspection is opt-in and clearly separated from playable information. Critical historical events are never dropped to enforce a log budget; routine events are aggregated deterministically.

### 13.2 State and determinism

`CampaignState` holds map fields/edges, entity tables, civilization ledgers, Gods/corpses, policies, tech, diplomacy, observation memories, commands still pending, deterministic RNG stream states, event allocator and history indexes. The renderer, camera, audio playback clocks, decoded textures and DOM focus are not game state.

Use integer/fixed-point quantities, stable iteration order, explicit integer rounding and bounded products below JavaScript's safe integer ceiling. Gameplay must not use `Math.random`, `Date.now`, frame delta, asynchronous completion order or GPU readback. Seed streams are separately named WORLD, STARTS, ECOLOGY and AI-PERSONALITY so adding decorative foliage does not change wars. Random access uses the CG-XOR32-v1 and stream initialization specified in Section 16.7; execute its golden vectors at M01. Rendering uses a separate non-authoritative seed.

A committed state hash is SHA-256 over canonical, sorted serialized state excluding presentation and wall-clock metadata. It includes observation memories and RNG state. Keep command logs and a state checkpoint every twenty turns for replay, plus the most recent committed turn for crash recovery. Serialization describes fields explicitly; never serialize a Three.js scene as a save.

### 13.3 Storage and migration

Use IndexedDB database `civic-godstorm`, separate stores for campaign metadata, snapshots, command segments, event segments and approved asset-build identity. Settings may use localStorage, with failure-tolerant defaults. A save envelope contains `formatVersion, simulationVersion, contentHash, campaignId, turn, stateHash, payloadLength, checksum`. Save only at committed boundaries; an interrupted resolution restarts from the previous committed boundary and submitted command set, never from half-applied damage.

Maintain five rotating autosaves plus five user slots. Commit data and active-save pointer in one transaction. On quota failure, keep the current in-memory game, show `NOT SAVED`, offer file export and do not delete a good save automatically. Import accepts a bounded file <=50 MiB, rejects path/script content, validates schema/ranges/checksum, and loads into a new slot without overwriting the source. Export is a versioned compressed JSON archive with a `.cgsave` extension; CompressionStream availability is probed, with an uncompressed JSON encoding flag fallback.

Migrations are pure `n -> n+1` transforms run against a copy, with fixtures for every supported version. Unknown future versions fail with an explanatory message and export preservation. Content manifests retain logical IDs/aliases for supported saves; deleted or renamed gameplay IDs require an explicit migration, not a missing-key default. Worker or context loss must not invalidate the last committed save.

## 14. Technical architecture and decision record

### 14.1 Frozen technology choices

TypeScript strict mode; Vite build/dev server; React for semantic DOM interface; Three.js `WebGLRenderer` for the 3D world; native Canvas 2D for a small minimap; native IndexedDB/Web Audio/Pointer Events; Vitest for deterministic/unit tests and Playwright for browser journeys. Node scripts own data validation and asset integration; Blender background Python owns reference-constrained 3D build/export where required. No external physics engine, ECS framework, global-state library, component-3D binding layer, pathfinding library or procedural-world framework is required.

Use one npm workspace/package and one lockfile. At M00 resolve a mutually compatible stable release set, record exact versions and licenses, and pin them in the lockfile and toolchain file before any milestone implementation. This is dependency verification, not permission to change stack. Use a maintained Node LTS supported by the chosen Vite/test versions; a user's unrelated global Node installation must not be changed silently. Exact installed version facts cannot be asserted during a documentation-only pass.

### 14.2 Comparative architecture decisions

**AD-01: hybrid browser architecture selected.** Three.js supplies scene/camera/glTF/animation infrastructure for the actual 3D asset requirements; React/DOM carries dense accessible management surfaces without recreating text controls in a GPU canvas. The canonical 3D toolkit's hybrid strategy profile is the direct project precedent. Three.js documents `WebGLRenderer` as a WebGL2 renderer [E1] and supplies glTF loading [E2]. This is a rationale for selection, not proof of the game's performance.

**AD-02: reject a new full-engine/export layer for V1.** Godot's web export is a credible alternative with an editor and engine lifecycle, but introduces an additional export/runtime boundary for this TypeScript/DOM-heavy, headless-testable strategy design [E3]. No existing game engine is present in the inspected empty target repository. If future inspection finds working code, preservation outranks this greenfield assumption and a conflict must be reported.

**AD-03: reject raw WebGPU/custom rendering.** No product requirement needs GPU compute or exact low-level pipeline ownership. Keep WebGL2 and avoid a second rendering backend. Unsupported WebGL2 yields an honest requirement/recovery screen, not a silently inferior fake 3D build.

**AD-04: bounded morphological composition.** Spore's official prototype collection illustrates separating creature/body and simulation experiments rather than beginning with a finished whole-world game [E4]. CIVIC GODSTORM adapts only the practice of isolated capability proofs; it does not claim to reproduce Spore's technology or arbitrary creature freedom. Four anatomical templates plus constrained slots are a deliberate reduction of rigging risk.

**AD-05: source-image asset pipeline.** Current Arena help describes image generation, file work and sandbox tools in Agent Mode, and GitHub branch/PR workflows [E5,E6]. It does not establish production rigged-GLB output. Therefore Arena delivers authored sources; Opus must perform and prove actual geometry, skinning, conversion and integration. No paid image-to-3D service is a hidden fallback.

**AD-06: turn resolution baseline first, worker only after evidence.** Pure simulation functions run headlessly and initially in time-sliced main-thread jobs with UI progress; if the matched large-map benchmark exceeds 50 ms main-thread blocking or the target turn latency, move that exact resolver behind a dedicated Worker adapter. The wire schema is fixed below; SharedArrayBuffer and OffscreenCanvas remain excluded. This follows the toolkit's measurement gate rather than introducing concurrency by fashion.

### 14.4 Resolver boundary and lifecycle

`ResolveRequest = {jobId, stateVersion, stateHash, rulesHash, seedState, snapshot, commands}`. `ResolveResult = {jobId, baseStateHash, nextStateHash, nextState, events, diagnostics}`. Accept only the matching active job and base hash. Cancellation discards an uncommitted result; it cannot leave half-applied state. A worker crash falls back to retrying the pure resolver from the committed snapshot once, then reports a reproducible blocker.

Main-thread yielding must not affect sorted order or RNG. AI budgets use operation counts. No sim/render shared mutable objects. Observations are generated from committed authoritative state, not guessed from meshes. Frame updates interpolate only presentation position/posture; order outcome and attack timing come from resolved impulses.

The application owns listeners, observers, workers, object URLs, audio nodes and GPU resource registry. Scene detachment is not disposal. Asset references are counted; shared geometry/material/texture is released only at zero references. Pause on visibility loss; no wall-clock campaign advance. Resume resets presentation timers. Context loss preserves simulation/save, stops render/audio and offers reconstruction; rebuild resources from verified manifests, then verify current state hash unchanged. A renderer failure cannot become a God death.

### 14.5 Rendering and asset integration

Partition terrain into 8 x 8-cell chunks; rebuild only dirty chunk meshes and shared-edge seams. Instanced props are grouped by model/material/LOD/chunk, with instance-to-entity maps for picking. Three.js instancing is an available mechanism [E7], not a claim that all skinned Gods can use it. Gods use individual actual skins; static props and unit-group proxies use shared geometry. Avoid transparency-heavy full-screen effects.

Use a restrained rough, vertex-colored material treatment, one directional light plus hemisphere fill and one bounded shadow map on medium/high. Runtime visual styles may quantize lighting via a documented material adapter; portable GLB materials retain a readable rough PBR approximation. No baked photoreal normal/albedo texture set is required. Base colors and UI/key art are sRGB; masks, roughness, normals and numeric data are linear/non-color. No authored font files are supplied; use platform system font stacks and measured layout fallbacks.

Build outputs have physical hashes, bounds, triangles, LOD presence, color roles and discovered skin/node/clip metadata. Required planned names are validated against actual exports before runtime uses them. Procedural animation drives bones from semantic rig metadata and resolved paths; it does not rely on invented GLB clip names. Every character pass produces the actual GLB, editable recipe/source, validation JSON, tested inspector and a turntable fallback. Section 18 defines the finishing gate.

### 14.6 Audio, offline distribution and security

Web Audio begins only after user interaction. Synth buses are UI, environment, movement, conflict and music; master mute is always available. God scale uses low-frequency filtered impacts with strict level limits, not painfully loud sound. No audio is necessary for understanding state; captions and visual cues duplicate alerts. The finite audio recipe set is in Section 17 and is wholly procedural, with no Arena audio request.

Build a static site with relative asset base for `/Civic_Godstorm/`; no SPA routes require server rewrite. A versioned service worker caches the shell and verified runtime asset pack after a successful complete download, with an explicit offline-ready indicator. Interrupted pack updates retain the old usable version. Never cache private credentials or raw development source sheets in the shipping pack. Test offline only after the pack has been acquired; first-visit offline is an honest unavailable state.

GitHub Pages is the intended eventual host, but this planning task does not publish a game or configure deployment. Publishing is a separate explicit release action after M14. CI/build commands must not deploy as a side effect. Imported saves and data are untrusted: validate ranges/size/schema and render text as text. No dynamic code evaluation, arbitrary downloaded scripts, CDN runtime dependency or secrets in frontend bundles.

### 16.2 Exact coordinate and footprint rules

Use pointy-top hexes with odd-row offset storage. For stored column `c` and row `r`, axial `q = c - (r - (r & 1))/2`; inverse `c = q + (r - (r & 1))/2`. Normalize `c mod width`, preserve `r`, reject rows outside `[0,height)`. World centers are `x = 32*(q+r/2)`, `z = 16*sqrt(3)*r`, `y = heightToU(elevation)`. These floats are presentation only. Heading directions in axial order are `(1,0),(0,1),(-1,1),(-1,0),(0,-1),(1,-1)`. Rotate an offset once with `(q,r) -> (-r,q+r)`. Model forward +Z maps to heading 0 by a +90-degree Y rotation.

Height bands map to `0.25 U * max(0,elevation-seaLevel)` for terrain presentation, capped at 40 U above local water. Regional change costs are clamped to valid bounds, never credited as free resources beyond reserves.

`Impact {sourceId, causeId, cells, edges, intensity, kind, consent, observedForecast}` updates in order: structure damage -> ecological disturbance -> stress/hydrology invalidation -> legal displacement -> treaty incidents -> historical consequences. Intensity is in 0..1000; ordinary heavy passage adds `10*mass`, forceful passage multiplies by 3/2, careful passage by 1/2, each rounded down. Road damage is the final intensity; other ground structure damage is `floor(intensity/2)` for an unreinforced obstruction. A capacity-compatible Godway multiplies passage intensity by 1/4, rounded down; insufficient capacity remains illegal. Movement must stop before unconsented predicted civilian harm. Disasters follow the same transaction, with warnings and no requirement to have player consent for a naturally caused result.

Four disaster modes are FIRE (dryness>=800, biomass>=400, heat source; consumes 100 biomass/turn, spreads to one eligible neighbor), FLOOD (flow>capacity; 80 structure damage, moisture+200), SUBSIDENCE (stress>=800 for two turns; elevation-8, 100 damage) and STORM (forecast weather band; air blocked, shipping movement cost+1). Warnings appear one turn before threshold resolution when observable. Infrastructure reinforcement, watershed work and evacuations change outcomes. No additional disaster-only art is required beyond the registered procedural effects.

### 16.5 Civic accounting closure

Population is in milli-population; one full unit represents 1,000 people. A job uses 1,000 milli-population; fractional jobs yield proportionally, rounded down with per-ledger carried remainder. Basic household upkeep is 2 FOOD/full population/turn. A founding party costs one population unit from a source settlement and carries a 4-turn ration supply, 12 TIMBER and 8 STONE; founding takes one resolution turn on a legal unclaimed or consented site. The party is a convoy, not an unlisted unique character model. It can found while its God is absent or dead.

A settlement has a non-working minimum of one-quarter of population for children/elder care/governance; available labor is `floor(populationMilli*750/1000)` milli-workers, with fractional assignments/remainders retained. Starting five population therefore supports three whole jobs plus 750 milli-workers; the guaranteed opening is two farm workers (8 FOOD) plus the settlement base yield (2 FOOD), leaving one whole worker for materials and fractional capacity for construction. No starting food deficit is hidden by a disabled system. Initial local available farmland and biomass support this assignment.

Welfare is the weighted mean `45% food coverage + 20% housing coverage + 15% health service + 10% safety + 10% participation`, each 0..1000. Coverage ratios clamp at 1000; no surplus buys infinite happiness. Disease is a local health burden from contamination, not a separate contagion pathogen simulator. Health service starts at 500 without an infirmary, rises to 800 with staffed civic treatment and 1000 with medicine; divine treatment is tagged separately. Food coverage is actual consumed/required. Housing is provided capacity/population. Safety is 1000 except visible local war/disaster hazards and their trailing four-turn trauma; participation follows governance policy and legitimacy. Welfare never uses an arbitrary God-alive bonus.

Base housing capacity is 6 population in a core; every staffed/maintained DWELLING parcel adds 6, capped at 32. Packed settlements retain capacity <=8. Employment quotas are player configurable and priority-based; scarce inputs are allocated deterministically by declared priority then site ID. Construction jobs produce 4 work/worker/turn. Never count the same worker in production and construction. Maintenance that cannot be paid degrades integrity by 5/turn and disables service at integrity<25, with a warning before the first loss.

Base claims extend one ring from a settlement. A staffed HALL plus T-INS-1 expands cultural claims to two rings; T-INS-3 to three. Contested claims compare `100*population + 50*staffedHall + 25*localHeritage`, minus 20 per path step from center, with at most 20 points/turn change to prevent flicker. Physical occupation, passage rights and political ownership remain separate layers. A God does not claim land by simply being there; it creates a bodily obstruction and potential negotiation. LocalHeritage is capped at 20 unique events. Claims at equal influence remain contested rather than silently changing owner by ID.

God trust changes by +5 per fully supplied, protected rest turn; +10 per successful treatment; -10 per excess BIO unit forcibly extracted; -20 per preventable severe injury; -30 per confirmed civilian-collateral event, clamped 0..1000. Public burden increases from household shortages attributable to divine provisioning, displacement and destructive passage and decays by 5/turn without new harm. These terms feed the faction shift model, not involuntary God actions.

### 16.7 Frozen random, settlement and policy rules

The simulation PRNG is `CG-XOR32-v1`: unsigned 32-bit state `x`; perform `x ^= x << 13`, then `x ^= x >>> 17`, then `x ^= x << 5`, truncating to unsigned 32 bits after each operation; return/store the result. State zero is replaced on initialization by `0x6D2B79F5`. This is a game random stream, not a security primitive. Seed input is a 1-64-character ASCII string using letters, digits, dot, underscore, colon or hyphen. Each named stream is initialized by FNV-1a 32-bit hashing of `seed + ':' + streamName`: offset `2166136261`, XOR each ASCII byte, multiply by `16777619` modulo 2^32 using exact 32-bit multiplication. Coordinate noise hashes the same seed plus integer cell/octave labels; decorative noise uses a separate RENDER namespace that is never serialized as gameplay RNG.

For a bounded integer `0..n-1`, reject stream draws >=`floor(4294967296/n)*n`, then take modulo n. Stable sorted candidate enumeration precedes any draw. Golden successive outputs from state 1 are `270369, 67634689, 2647435461, 307599695, 2398689233`; M01 executes them in every target runtime. No algorithm choice remains open. SHA-256 canonical state hashing is independent of this random stream.

Settlement cores must be at least three wrapped hex steps apart. Founding and autonomy never exceed the 96-site world envelope; conquered cities occupy existing site IDs. Neutral autonomous sites count against world population and site budgets but are not additional full AI civilizations for the six-civilization planner limit. A displaced cohort that cannot obtain an active convoy slot remains a real stationary waiting cohort at a legal evacuation cell, shown with the existing convoy/canopy presentation. Merge by owner/current cell/destination while preserving population, cargo and cause IDs; never delete people because a renderer or moving-group budget is full.

### 17.10 Composition and ownership closure

**God seams and rigs.** Each family has its own continuous torso topology and semantic junction layout. Limb repetition is Q=4, H=6; S is one deforming axial body with optional two fins; M has two fins or two wings. No humanoid skeleton substitution. The module fitter records reference landmarks and junction rings, limits local anisotropic junction scaling to 15%, welds continuous skin, matches normals/color/weights and preserves the worst-view silhouette. ## 18. Production pipeline, intake gates and asset feasibility

### 18.1 Separate content from machinery

Arena creates the 121 accepted authored source images. Opus implements the conversion/integration system and 112 derived assets plus 52 procedural recipes. It may use Blender Python, continuous subdivision/lofted cages, controlled silhouette fitting, procedural UV-free vertex materials, skinning and export. It may not generate alternate authored pictures or use stock/marketplace/third-party icons to evade the source boundary. Primitive shapes are permitted only as unmistakable development placeholders or as geometry-building intermediates; they do not automatically satisfy an organic reference.

The feasible V1 strategy is deliberately source-reference-led stylization: four continuous anatomical templates with constrained junctions, vertex color, finite equipment/building modules and procedural animation. It does **not** promise arbitrary image-to-perfect-rig automation. The first real Q God is the feasibility gate. If Opus cannot reproduce its approved silhouette and deformation with available local tools, stop that milestone, preserve the approved references and evidence, and request an explicit production-method decision. Do not continue to a hundred nominal models or quietly flatten the game into sprites. There is no paid-service fallback hidden in this plan.

Before conversion, probe actual Blender, Python, CPU/GPU, memory, browser and package capabilities. On an 8 GiB laptop run one model conversion/render at a time; no large local generative network is required. Optional learned geometry may be used only as a licensed, locally executable hypothesis provider with explicit user approval, never as authority or a new recurring cost. The baseline does not depend on it.

### 18.2 State machine and manifest separation

The asset status machine is `SPECIFIED -> CANDIDATE -> AWAITING_APPROVAL -> APPROVED_SOURCE -> DERIVED_UNVERIFIED -> INTEGRATED_VERIFIED`; branches are `REJECTED` and `BLOCKED`. Direct key art moves from APPROVED_SOURCE through file/browser checks to INTEGRATED_VERIFIED without a model step. Procedural recipes require code tests and browser evidence rather than an Arena approval record for a nonexistent image.

At M00 compile Section 17 into `assets/specification.json`; it contains requirements, not success claims. `assets/provenance.json` records actual candidates, source approval, native dimensions and rights evidence. `assets/manifest.json` contains only actual verified shipping file facts and registered recipe IDs. `assets/missing.json` names unresolved specification IDs. These implementation-stage machine files do not become a fourth canonical planning document and are not created during this planning pass.

An unavailable asset reference is `AssetRef {id, status:'MISSING'}` rather than a guessed file URL. Development fallback is a checker/wireframe object bearing its literal ID. The same missing ID yields the same debug representation without touching simulation state. Production builds fail on required missing/unapproved assets; a network-corrupt file produces a recoverable error, never a false approved substitute.

### 18.3 Reference-to-model procedure

For each substantial model task create a small machine-readable TaskSpec, ReferenceSpec, ValidationPlan and CompletionContract. TaskSpec records stylized organic/hard-surface classification, intended use, required formats/rig/LODs. ReferenceSpec lists approved source IDs/revisions, required views and identity anchors. An identity anchor records anatomical count, shape/proportion, required views, permitted tolerance and veto severity. Prioritize the four body-family profiles, feeding apparatus, sensory counts, tapering tails, locomotor joint structure and exact distinctive negative spaces.

Work through: normalize references -> confirm cross-view consistency -> build continuous low-frequency geometry -> fit global mass/proportions -> fit identity landmarks -> render clay/silhouette views -> accept shape -> add deformation-aware loops -> construct actual joints and normalized skin -> pose test -> apply approved materials -> generate silhouette-preserving LODs -> export -> structural validation -> reimport -> actual browser inspector -> turntable and acceptance record.

Do not optimize a single flattering camera. Required diagnostics are front, left, rear, top and a rotating 3/4 view; deforming parts also show maximum ordinary bends, feeding, sleep, turn, locomotion and the family's exceptional mode. For a measured consistent reference, target silhouette IoU >=0.90 in each orthographic view after declared alignment and landmark error <=3% of body length. For a deliberately non-projectable source, do not fake those numbers: record conflict, obtain approval of a corrected reference/fit, then freeze that measurable target. Any missing digit/eye/tail/wing or broken body-family identity vetoes acceptance even when averages pass.

Skin weights sum to 1 within 1e-4, no NaN/inverted bind transforms, no unintended detached skin, no gross joint volume collapse, no missing body on any required LOD. If automatic remeshing is used, it is an intermediate, not proof of animation topology. Fine surface detail is prohibited until global silhouette passes.

### 18.4 Candidate revisions, reproducibility and delivery

Maintain immutable `accepted_revision`, a candidate derived from it, source hashes and a localized change reason. Candidate failure never replaces the accepted source or model. Promote only after improved target criteria with no critical regression. Two unsuccessful localized revisions of the same identity issue trigger review, not another unbounded remodeling loop. This asset revision rule does not waive the overall one-primary-pass/one-repair-pass milestone limit.

Record the Blender/tool versions and deterministic recipe parameters. A rerun must reproduce logical topology, bounds and semantic metadata; byte-identical GLB is a target only when the exporter itself is deterministic. Hash comparisons must distinguish byte reproducibility from geometric equivalence. No runtime code binds a planned bone/socket/clip name before the actual export metadata has been inspected and validated.

Run the Khronos glTF validator or an equivalent documented glTF2 structural validator and retain its actual report. Reimport the exact delivered GLB. The offline inspector must load that file/embedded byte sequence, show nonblank model pixels and exercise orbit/zoom/reset, reference cameras, locomotion/pose state and LOD switch with the network disabled. A blank viewer shell or GIF alone is not delivery. The actual GLB remains the primary model artifact; diagnostics and turntable are evidence, not substitutes.

The exact source allocation is duplicated in the Arena prompt. B00 approves global style; B01 supplies the first Q God; B02 supplies first civic appearance. Stop at each approval gate before mass generation. B03-B06 complete remaining God grammar and lifecycle references; B07 completes civic architecture/motifs; B08 completes units/environment; B09-B11 complete identity and glyph language; B12 creates title art from accepted designs. Batches follow dependency and visual continuity, not an arbitrary number of images.

Implementation may proceed with conspicuous placeholders through M02. Later missing batches are tracked by exact IDs. Asset intake may run alongside an implementation milestone but may not bypass its acceptance gate. Only the minimum next dependent batch is requested; do not spend generation quota reproducing already approved IDs.

The initial visible baseline is deliberately small: one Q, one civic motif, one city, roads/farms, one civilian proxy, visible ecological change and a playable God order. Premium feasibility is judged there before full variant production. Three good interacting systems and one convincing body prove more than thirty disconnected thumbnails.

### 19.1 Defined validation command surface

M00 establishes and documents these commands, matching actual script implementations before running them: `npm run typecheck`, `npm run lint`, `npm run test:unit`, `npm run test:sim`, `npm run test:assets`, `npm run test:e2e`, `npm run test:campaigns`, `npm run bench`, `npm run build`, `npm run preview`, and `npm run validate:release`. The commands are target interfaces, not claims they already exist. Tests/benchmarks accept a seed, map size, scenario, turn count and output directory. Exit nonzero on failed required gates; never swallow failures to keep an agent loop advancing.

The validation ladder is: repository/source inspection -> schema/type/lint -> deterministic units/invariants -> system integration/save round-trip -> physical asset validation -> real browser boot -> actual player interaction -> visual/reference review -> representative performance/lifecycle -> release manifest and clean-worktree review. Earlier evidence cannot substitute for later evidence. Run the relevant suffix when the changed surface warrants it; do not rerun a huge unrelated suite to conceal an untested critical journey.
## 20. Test strategy and representative campaign proofs

### 20.1 Automated test layers

**Unit tests:** quantity rounding/remainders; PRNG golden vectors; offset/axial/wrap transforms; mask rotation; region health/costs; exposure updates; growth eligibility; tech prerequisites; policy cooldown; treaty notice/expiry; finite corpse reserves; event ordering; canonical serialization. Every confirmed recurring defect becomes a regression fixture, not an instruction to be more careful.

**Property/invariant tests:** quantities and health are bounded; population is conserved across recruitment/displacement/demobilization except named births/deaths; cargo is conserved across dispatch/arrival/seizure/spoilage; one God identity persists through death; zero resurrection; each active capability has a visible registered module; incompatible anatomy is rejected; footsteps cannot skip blocked masks; observer state never points into hidden true state; AI and human share legality; a repeated command ID cannot spend twice; history causes point backward; render calls cannot change state hash.

**Deterministic simulation:** replay a recorded command sequence with different visual frame rates (15/30/60/120), presentation skip settings, asset load orders and main-thread yield schedules. The committed hash sequence must match. Repeat Node headless, Chromium, Firefox and WebKit/Safari execution on the release matrix. A cross-runtime mismatch is a real defect; do not label a fixed turn loop deterministic by assumption. Rendering-only randomness is excluded from hashes and cannot influence decisions.

**Pathfinding:** every rigid mask in all six rotations at both cylinder seams; serpent body trail and reversal; narrow water channel rejection; carrying/landing clearance; missing bridge; capacity downgrade; terrain change invalidating route; new observation stopping a path without leaking earlier knowledge; simultaneous swap/meeting; God-God projected collision; impossibility distinguished from search-budget exhaustion. For small graphs compare A* results to exhaustive shortest paths. Flow/routing tests include a route with legal passage but no feeding rights, and a fed God that cannot physically reach its stock.

**Economy/4X:** opening food ledger; failed recipe inputs; build queue worker conservation; household-vs-God provisioning priority; trade escrow theft/delivery/return; merchant capacity; surplus spoilage; tax suspension on shortage; supply-starved army; occupation without annexation; paid repair; price/fee repeat exploits; archive independence after death; no negative coin-to-material conversion. Compare service outage projections with actual no-new-action runs and label expected uncertainty from enemies/climate.

**God behavior:** every command and failure code, owner override of automation, hibernation early wake, growth blocked by space, fatigue threshold, damaged feeding conversion, lost limb, membrane regrowth, irreversible ossification, starvation, marine/air/subsurface death and gallery evacuation. Check that biology constrains physical possibility/cost without making an AI take over the player's actor.

**AI:** hidden-state noninterference pairs share identical observations but different secret enemy locations/resources; AI commands must match until a new observation differs. Audit all mutations through command validation. Run separately with each legal locomotor pairing and early death. Report opening starvation, illegal-order rate, blocked-path recovery, deliberate encounter rate, service redundancy and victory participation. Difficulty may enlarge planning horizon/candidate budget, not true-state access or resources.

**Save/load:** hash round-trips at planning/committed boundaries, interruption during resolution, worker crash, interrupted IDB transaction, quota denial, truncated/corrupt import, decompression bomb limits, unknown future schema, valid older migration, stale asset manifest, content ID migration and export/import across target browsers. A `Saved` toast requires successful transaction completion. An import must never overwrite the original file or only good slot.

**Asset validation:** registry uniqueness, all dependency references, no graph cycles, exact class/profile counts, file existence, image dimensions/alpha/color role, source status, physical SHA-256, GLB structure, actual bounds/material/skin/LOD metadata, pose deformation, worst-view identity, atlas frame bounds/bleed, missing-asset fallback, no release placeholder, viewer-current-revision equality and source/runtime pack separation. Reference completion, model completion and integration completion are separately counted.

**Performance/lifecycle:** the named stress fixture, sparse map, close-to-God view, corpse city, far overlay and 128-convoy scene; frame/turn/path distributions; ten transitions; repeated resizing and DPR changes; 200% zoom; background/foreground; context loss; audio suspension; failed asset decode; stale async result; offline pack interruption. Measured failures drive optimizations in M12, not speculative frameworks.

### 20.3 Browser journeys and adversarial agency checks

J01: new campaign -> choose one of three valid starts -> find God within one interaction -> select route -> inspect footprint -> commit -> observe actual consequences. J02: plan an unconsented destructive route -> receive accurate warning -> choose safe route -> later deliberately consent to a different harmful route. J03: persistent migration -> newly blocked bridge -> legal halt -> direct player override -> reroute. J04: city growth -> construction/material shortage -> actual trade consignment -> arrival -> visible new district. J05: valid Godform molt -> genuine geometry/capability change -> save/reload. J06: hibernation and independently functioning civilization. J07: God death -> gallery/refugee accounting -> memorial dock -> twenty further turns -> corpse inspection. J08: treaty conflict -> avoided God encounter -> explicit later escalation -> map damage and displacement. J09: keyboard-only and actual tablet portrait/landscape management without losing God access. J10: production offline pack -> network off -> play/save/export/import -> renderer loss/recovery with matching state hash.

Attempt to break the fantasy deliberately: issue a legal low-trust move; cancel automation; withhold a powerful strike; build a city that rejects divine services; grow into an invalid footprint; move a visually winged God that has no wing capability; feed from an unreachable warehouse; turn off animation during combat; hide an enemy in a state-paired AI fixture. Legal player choices must remain choices, impossible physics must remain impossible, and presentation must never manufacture a capability.

### 20.4 Human quality gates

Five observed playtesters or five separately documented user sessions should be able to explain a God-caused route/settlement decision, identify one civilization-caused body development, recover from a supply warning, and describe an event chain from the history interface. This is a research target, not an automated metric substituting for people. When actual external playtesting is unavailable, mark human usability confidence unverified and obtain the user's explicit narrower RC acceptance; do not fabricate testers.

The God must remain non-interchangeable with an ordinary super-unit: removing its ecological footprint/services must measurably change at least settlement placement, transport and diplomacy in the tested campaign. Two contrasting forms must change legal map access, provisioning and military risk, not only an icon/stat. Idle God care should not demand more than the cadence targets in Section 2.4 during a stable ten-turn period. A dead-God campaign must remain an engaging 4X with new decisions, not a prolonged punishment screen.

## 22. Production governance, change control and planning completion

### 22.1 Feasible scheduling and scope protection

This is a staged production program, not an instant AAA promise. The critical path is M00-M03 feasibility, M05 vertical slice, M06/M08 morphology-and-AI competence, M09 asset closure and M12-M14 measured release quality. Most authored volume is compact reusable source modules/glyphs, not unique full creatures. Broad geometry fitting and UI polish still require review; no source image generator eliminates that work.

Allocate effort by evidence rather than a fictitious calendar: finish the first real God before mass conversion; finish the God-death slice before content expansion; profile a real late-game state before introducing a worker; obtain actual tablet evidence before claiming touch support. Pause at approvals, quotas or unavailable tools. No credit-card purchase, paid API or installation requiring administrative changes is preauthorized by this plan.

Document consequential decisions in implementation-stage ADRs linked from the operational state. A proposed deviation must name the failed requirement, actual evidence, smallest intervention, affected assets/save schemas, rollback and required approval. Do not silently migrate the renderer, add runtime AI or expand the authored count. Existing verified implementation wins preservation until a conflict is deliberately resolved, not because a new agent prefers a cleaner scaffold.

### 22.2 Repository discipline for the planning package

Verified initial public repository metadata reported default branch `main` and no branch refs. A separate machine-side `git ls-remote` also returned no refs. These were point-in-time observations; recheck before pushing. The planning workspace is a fresh repository and contains only the three required Markdown outputs. No source image, model, package manifest, game code, dependency lockfile, simulation test or implementation status file is part of this documentation commit.

Before mutation inspect root, branch, remotes, local changes, remote HEAD/branches and repository ownership. Initialize only an uninitialized workspace. The target origin is `https://github.com/westkitty/Civic_Godstorm.git`. If a bundle or safe temporary transport origin is used, record that known transport and replace only that known value with the target after checking it; never overwrite an unexplained remote. Fetch when possible. Stage the three named documents explicitly, inspect staged filenames/diff/whitespace, then commit with `docs: establish Civic Godstorm production architecture`.

Push a normal non-forced `main` update only while remote ancestry is compatible with the inspected state. If remote work appeared or diverged, stop and report the actual refs rather than resetting, rebasing unknown work, force-pushing or overwriting it. Verify the resulting remote SHA and exact file set. A local commit does not prove a push; a reported push does not prove the remote contains the expected files until checked. Stop after the planning handoff; do not run either execution prompt.

The planning package is ready when architecture/mechanics/scope are explicit; player God control is first-class; Godform mechanics and visible modules correspond; variation changes real strategy; death preserves civilization; AI parity and observation limits are specified; every authored/derived/procedural requirement is registered; all milestones have gates; both prompts can run from this package without conversation history; and the three documents have actually been safely committed and pushed.
