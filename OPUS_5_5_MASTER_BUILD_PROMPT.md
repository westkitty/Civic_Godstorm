## 2. Product invariant: the God is directly controlled

Build a genuine civilization-scale 4X on a beautiful stylized 3D strategic map. The player commands one persistent giant organism from campaign start until permanent death. It has physical multi-cell occupancy, actual food/rest needs, player-issued movement/actions, lawful pathfinding, wounds, meaningful morphology and real effects on cities, routes, ecology, diplomacy and history.

The civilization also has its own economy, population, research, institutions, armies and futures. Sleep, absence or death does not disable civilization management or end the campaign. Permanent remains retain the actual God's identity, location and morphology, and produce finite resources and new territorial decisions. Gods cannot respawn, teleport, secretly regenerate severed limbs or become AI-controlled mascots. Temperament affects pressure/cost, never the player's fundamental ability to command a legal action.

Your acceptance sentence is: **I chose what my God did, where it went, how I developed it, and how much my civilization relied on it. The consequences became history.** A super-unit with a talent menu does not satisfy it. A normal 4X with a separate God submenu does not satisfy it.
